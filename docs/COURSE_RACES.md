# Alien Sky Race / سباق السماء الغريبة and Summit Race / سباق القمة (2026-11-18)
Two owner requests (videos + prompts), one engine: game_src/course_race (sim.js pure, game.js three.js, alien.js / rhino.js). `npm test` (4 tests; a robot finishes every round of both games), `npm run build`.
- 3 rounds (qualify top 4, top 3, then win the final among 6 runners). Drag to steer, tap/JUMP to jump. Hits push you back, falls put you back on the ledge (nothing lost).
- Alien: floating islands, gaps, speed rings, opening/closing doors, meteors (red shadow warns), green tube shortcuts (+26 m), jetpack with 3 bursts (JET), rocket at the end.
- Rhino: helmeted rhino, gaps, breakaway gates (dive/jump through), spinning sweepers, swinging hammers, bounce pads, power-ups, golden gate at the end.
- Not copied exactly: the videos' art and course layouts (generated from a seed, drawn from shapes in code). NOT tested on a real phone; sound not heard by a person.

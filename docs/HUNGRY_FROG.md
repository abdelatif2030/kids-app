# «لعبة الضفدع الجائع» — Hungry Frog

On the second fun-games page («ألعاب أكتر»). Ages 3+ (age 3-4 easy: an extra heart, slower warnings).

## How it plays
- A funny frog on a lily pond: drag anywhere to hop, tap the pink button to LEAP (safe in the air, cooldown ring).
- The sticky tongue snaps by itself (a stretchy pink tube with a wobbly spring) at the nearest critter whose number
  is not bigger than the frog's. Eating adds the number (big ones add part of it) and the frog grows; "AIR SNAP!"
  bonus when catching while leaping, combos for quick bites.
- RED numbers are bigger: bully frogs and snakes aim first (a red dotted line) then lash; herons swoop (a shadow, then
  the beak). Being caught costs a heart; out of hearts = "GULPED!" (try again, nothing else lost).
- Three levels: Misty Dawn (grow to 15 to evolve, 26, then beat Big Bo the bully frog with a crown), Lily Marsh (fish
  jump, snakes, herons; boss Sly the Snake), Sunset Swamp (boss: the Grand Heron). The frog gets spots after evolving
  and a crown at the boss stage.

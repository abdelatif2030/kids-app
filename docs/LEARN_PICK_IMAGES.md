# صور شاشة الاختيارين («مستويات» و«اتعلّم») — برومتات (نفس شات ChatGPT)
دلوقتي الشاشة بترسم كل اختيار بالكود (سلالم لنجمة + كتاب مفتوح). لو بعتّ صور بالاسمين دول هتتركّب مكانهم أوتوماتيك:
`assets/games/learn_lang/mode_levels.webp` و `assets/games/learn_lang/mode_learn.webp` (خلفية شفافة، مربعة تقريبًا).

**Style base:** Polished kids' mobile-game art, cute 3D-cartoon look, rounded shapes, saturated friendly colours, family friendly (no blood, no weapons, no brands, no known cartoon characters).
**TECH:** ONE single image, pure flat white background #FFFFFF, no gradient, no floor, no cast shadows, no frame, no text, no letters, no numbers. Subject centred with a clear margin.

**MODE-LEVELS** — Style base. TECH. A small glossy winding path of four round stepping stones going up and to the right, ending at a big golden star on a little podium with a tiny flag; sparkles around; warm and inviting.
**MODE-LEARN** — Style base. TECH. A big open storybook with glowing pages, and small glossy objects floating out of it: a colourful letter block, a number block, a cute lion head, a red apple and a pencil; sparkles around; warm and inviting.

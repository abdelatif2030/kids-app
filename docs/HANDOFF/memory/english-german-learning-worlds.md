Owner (2026-11-26) wants English and German in the educational games with the same amount of content as Arabic, a language chooser and a level map per language (English 9 levels, German 10 incl. Umlaute), each word heard 3 times (word, slow, sentence), recordings made from the CSV word lists (docs/ENGLISH_WORDLIST.csv 433 words, docs/GERMAN_WORDLIST.csv 437 words) and pictures from docs/ENGLISH_GERMAN_IMAGE_PROMPTS.md. Same ten games with a learn-language parameter, not new games; Arabic keeps the instructions/praise. Nothing is built in the app yet. Full plan: docs/HANDOFF/README.md section 11.

**Why:** the owner wants children to really learn the languages, not just hear them. **How to apply:** pilot one level (English Animals in «أطعم الحيوان») end to end first; German text needs a native check; never use Fatima/device TTS for English/German words.

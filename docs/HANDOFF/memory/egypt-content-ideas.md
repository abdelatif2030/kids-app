Owner wants every fun game's levels to teach Egyptian civilisation and famous places in a kid-friendly way (backdrops + level names), plus world landmarks. Collected so far (2026-11-25):
Pyramids of Giza + Sphinx, Cairo Tower, Nile + felucca, Kasr El-Nile bridge, Cairo Citadel, Cairo airport, Qaitbay Citadel (Alexandria corniche), Red Sea (Hurghada/Sharm), Siwa oasis, White Desert, Luxor + Karnak + Abu Simbel + Philae, Aswan (Nile, Nubian houses), Saint Catherine (Sinai mountain), Grand Egyptian Museum, Banha villas corniche, Dream Park and Aqua Park (famous Egyptian amusement/water parks), Khan El-Khalili, Eid night lanterns, Sinbad/Arabian nights; also take the signature thing of each governorate/city and show it kid-friendly. Also police/army/ambulance/fire vehicles in racing games.
**How to apply:** when writing a level backdrop prompt, pick a place from this list that fits the game's theme and not yet used in that game; name the level after it in simple Egyptian Arabic. After finishing the listed ones, search the web for more landmarks and keep going.
Used so far: moto_race (Alexandria, Red Sea, Nile bridges, Giza, Luxor, GEM), carnival_race (Nile funfair, Alexandria pier, Eid night, Paris, Sinbad, Snow).

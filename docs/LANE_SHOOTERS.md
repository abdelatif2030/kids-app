# Squad Shooter / فرقة الجنود and Archer Bridge / رماة الجسر (2026-11-18)
Two owner requests (videos + prompts) built on ONE engine: game_src/lane_shooter (sim.js pure, game.js three.js, squad.js / archers.js entries). `npm test` (6 tests; a robot clears all 8 levels), `npm run build` -> assets/games/squad_shooter and archer_bridge.
- Squad: cartoon soldiers on a night industrial road. Archers: cartoon archers on a stone bridge over lava, skeleton enemies.
- Drag to steer; the squad fires by itself. Number gates (+/-, shooting raises the number), numbered cages (break = free soldiers), weapon crates (4 levels), enemies (touch = lose soldiers), a boss wall at the end. Coins buy bow/rifle and rank upgrades (menu shop). 8 levels each. Nothing ends the game for good: a fallen squad just retries.
- Not copied: exact enemy kinds and art of the videos (drawn from shapes in code). NOT tested on a real phone; sound not heard by a person.

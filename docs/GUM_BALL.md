# «كرة العلكة العملاقة» — Gum Ball Roll

Card 15 on the fun-games page. Ages 3+ (age 3-4 get more time).

## How it plays
- A small pink ball of sticky gum: drag anywhere to roll. Anything up to 60% of the ball's size sticks to it (it
  shows on the ball) and the ball grows; things a little too big are nudged aside; things much bigger are walls (a
  bump, a little gum falls off). Goals: 1.3 m (street stuff), 3.2 m, 6.6 m (cars), 16 m (buses), 22 m (buildings),
  45 m, 60 m. 2:00 on the clock; there is no losing: stars at 20 / 40 / 60 m, best size kept.
- The city: a terracotta pedestrian plaza with clutter (candy, apples, rubber ducks, hot dogs, pretzels, bananas,
  balls, teddies, cones), umbrella tables, food carts, bikes, bins, hydrants, red stairs, colourful buildings with
  billboards; streets with cars, taxis, police cars, food trucks, red double-decker buses, fire trucks; blocks of
  shops, buildings, towers and skyscrapers; parks with trees. Each kind is one instanced mesh (fast on phones).

## Like the video
Three dark pills (ball diameter, next size goal with a bar, time left), "+Hot Dog" pops, "BIGGER! Now roll up ...",
pink trail spots, a light-blue title card. Sound: funky city tune, rolling rumble that deepens with size, pops (lower
for bigger things), squeaks, honks, sirens, bus horns, building rumbles, bumps, BIGGER fanfare, last-10-seconds ticks,
time-up whistle.

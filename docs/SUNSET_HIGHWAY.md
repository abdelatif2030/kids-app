# Sunset Highway / طريق الغروب (2026-11-18)
Owner request from a video: red sports car, palm-lined coastal highway at sunset, race the clock, overtake the rival and keep the lead. game_src/highway_race (`npm test` 5 tests; a robot wins all 3 stages), three.js.
- 4 lanes, busy traffic (cars and trucks), the road bends along the shore, sea and palms, sun disc. Drag to steer; NITRO (3 charges, +1 every 500 m). A bump only slows the car for a moment. Stages: Beach Sunset, Dusk Corniche, Harbour Night. Win = reach the line before time runs out AND ahead of the blue rival.
- Not copied exactly: the video's car models and scenery are drawn from shapes in code. NOT tested on a real phone; sound not heard by a person.

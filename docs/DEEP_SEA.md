# «أسماك الأعماق» — Deep Sea Fish

The wide card at the bottom of the fun-games page. Ages 4+ (age 4 easy: an extra life, more time).

## How it plays
- A small fish (blue with a yellow stripe): drag to swim, tap the red button to DASH (cooldown ring). Eat creatures up
  to 90% of your size to grow (combos); anything 15% bigger that is a predator hunts you: a red "!" warns, then it
  lunges. A bite costs a life and a little size. Each level is timed: grow to the goal before the time runs out.
- Sunlit Reef (grow to 45 cm; bait balls of sardines, butterflyfish, pufferfish, the grouper), Kelp Forest (grow to
  110 cm and eat 3 squid; mackerel, barracuda), Deep Trench (grow to 250 cm, then devour the shark; lanternfish,
  tuna, anglerfish). Hunters wait a few seconds at the start and there are never many at once.

## Phone first
The start screen and the level card fit a phone screen with large text and buttons (in the video the card was cut off
with scroll bars); nothing needs scrolling.

## Like the video
Blue water, sand ripples, rock arches, corals, kelp, light rays, bubbles; lives pill, timer pill, pause; goal pill with
a fish and a bar; "Bait ball incoming" toast; "Combo x4"; red dash button. Sound: dreamy ocean tune (darker in the
trench, pounding for the shark), bubbles, gulps that climb with the combo, dash whoosh, warning alarm, lunge, bonk,
bait shimmer, squid ink, shark arrival.

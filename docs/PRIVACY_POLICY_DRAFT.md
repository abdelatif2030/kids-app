## In short
- The app has no ads, no analytics and no tracking. The only chat is the optional family chat with relatives a parent approved (OFF unless the owner turns the online service on; see below).
- What a parent enters (the child's nickname, age, goals, progress, drawings, settings) is stored **only on this device**.
  We do not receive it.
- The app never asks for a child's real name, location or contacts. In this offline version the microphone is used only when a parent chooses to dictate a note (on-device recognition); the camera is not used. In a future online version the microphone and camera would be used only for family voice messages and calls that a parent switched on, and only when one starts.

## What is stored on the device
Child nickname and age chosen by the parent, finished activities and answers, drawings, notes, time used, settings and
the parent PIN (kept in the device's secure storage). Optional: an avatar picture, only if a parent allows it, kept on
this device. A parent can export or delete one child's data, or all data, in Parent settings.

## Videos
Lessons can play videos from a list of candidates that a **parent must review and approve** before any child can see them (the app has not verified them; the parent decides). A parent can also add one exact YouTube video link. They are played with YouTube's official player, so YouTube/Google may
process data about playback under their own policy ([link to Google's privacy policy]). We cannot remove YouTube's
branding or its own notices. The child cannot search YouTube or follow links out of the app.

## Device voice and printing
Spoken words use the voice built into the device. Printing or sharing a worksheet uses the system dialogs; the file is
created on the device.

## Optional teacher service (not active in this version)
If turned on in a future version, parents and teachers would create accounts (email, name, profile, bookings,
messages) stored with Google Firebase. Teachers are not vetted by the app. This policy will be updated before then.

## Optional family chat (not active in this version)
If switched on, a parent can invite relatives to exchange short messages and stickers with the child. Relatives create
an account (e-mail, name). Messages, the child's nickname and the relative's name are stored with Google Firebase. The
parent can read and delete every conversation and can remove a relative at any time. No photos, files or links. Voice messages (a short recording, kept for 14 days and then deleted), voice calls and video calls can each be switched on by the parent for an approved relative. Recordings are stored with Google Firebase until deleted; calls travel directly between the two devices: the voice travels directly between the two devices, is not recorded and is not stored; the microphone is used only during a call.

## Children
The app is designed for children with a parent present. Parents control the settings area with a PIN.

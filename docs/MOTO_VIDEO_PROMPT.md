# Prompt for the Motorbike Race test video (Instinct)

**Goal:** a short reference/asset clip of ONE rider on ONE bike with natural motorbike motion, seen from BEHIND (the game camera is behind the bike). We cut it into sprites/frames (lean left, straight, lean right, nitro, crash) and use it as a motion reference.

**Character (must match our art):** Zizo, a 7-year-old fox boy rider: orange fur with a white-tipped fluffy tail, big blue helmet with ear holes and a yellow lightning bolt, red racing jacket with white stripes, green gloves; blue sport bike with a yellow lightning stripe, twin exhausts, red tail light. Glossy cute 3D-cartoon style. (Attach our character sheet `moto_race-02` and rear-view sheet as image references.)

## Prompt (paste)
> A short looping clip, 6 seconds, 24 fps, 16:9, 1920x1080. A cute cartoon fox boy rider on a blue sport bike, seen strictly from BEHIND, centred, camera fixed at a low chase height. Plain flat bright green (#00FF00) background, no road, no shadow, no text, no logo, no other objects.
> Natural motorbike motion, in this order (1 second each): (1) riding straight at speed: slight suspension bounce, rear wheel spinning, the fox tail and scarf flutter, helmet steady; (2) smooth lean to the LEFT into a turn: bike and rider lean together about 25 degrees, handlebars turn a little, then ease back to upright; (3) smooth lean to the RIGHT the same way; (4) nitro boost: rider crouches low, two bright orange-yellow flames shoot from the twin exhausts, small speed streaks, bike stretches slightly forward; (5) cute crash: the bike wobbles, tips over, the rider tumbles softly with dizzy stars above the head (no injury, no blood), then pops back up smiling; (6) back to riding straight to loop.
> Weight and physics must feel real: bike leans before it turns, the rider shifts body weight, tyres stay grounded, no sliding or floating, no distortion of the character, same design in every frame.

## Delivery (what to ask for)
- **Format:** MP4 (H.264), 1920x1080, 24 fps, 6 s. Also a **WebM with transparent background** if possible; otherwise the green background above (we remove it).
- **Extra (optional, 1 file each):** the same clip from the SIDE view; and a 3-second loop of only "riding straight".
- No audio needed.

## If the first try is not natural, add
"Slow the motion slightly. The lean angle must never exceed 30 degrees. Keep the rider's body proportions identical in every frame. Wheels must keep rotating. The camera never moves."

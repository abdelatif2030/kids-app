# «سباق الطريق الساحلي» — Coastal Drive

On the second fun-games page («ألعاب أكتر»). Ages 4+ (age 4 easy).

## How it plays
- A sports car speeds along a busy four-lane coastal highway with no brakes: drag left/right (or tap a side, arrow keys)
  to change lanes. Pass close to cars, vans, pickups and trucks for NEAR MISSES; chain them for COMBO xN (coins are
  multiplied). Collect coin rows. NITRO (round button, fills with near misses) gives a burst of speed and flames.
- A crash costs a heart, slows the car and nudges the other car aside (nobody is hurt). Out of hearts = "Wrecked"
  (coins are kept). A level is won by reaching its distance with enough near misses.
- Six levels in three worlds: Azure Coast (palms, sand, sea, green hills, guard rail, street lamps), Red Canyon
  (mesas, cacti), Neon City (lit towers, neon lines). Traffic never forms a wall across three lanes; later levels
  have cars that change lanes after blinking. Garage: Striker, Arrow GT, Nightfall, Volt; upgrades engine, nitro,
  armor (LV 3 adds a heart).

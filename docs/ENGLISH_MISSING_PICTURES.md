**Style base (انسخه في أول كل برومت):** Polished kids' mobile-game art, cute 3D-cartoon look, glossy, rounded shapes, saturated friendly colours, family friendly (no blood, no weapons, no brands, no known cartoon characters).

**TECHNICAL RULES (انسخها في كل شيت):** TECHNICAL RULES (the picture is cut by machine): ONE single image, pure flat white background #FFFFFF, no gradient, no floor, no cast shadows, no frame, no text, no letters, no numbers. Exact grid, every cell the same size, each item centred with a clear margin, nothing touching the cell edge.
